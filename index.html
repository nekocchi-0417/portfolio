Hi, I’m Yue.

I’m a policy researcher from Singapore, now based in Berkeley. I care about keeping human agency as AI becomes more capable, and helping institutions recognise risks and act early.

Currently, I’m a research fellow at SPAR and the Odyssean Institute, studying AI commitments and how people reason about AI risks. Through the Better Futures fellowship, I’m also exploring AI-driven job displacement and economic agency.

Previously

  • Ministry of National Development: conducted research for national-level housing and retirement policies; piloted AI-assisted scenario planning research workflows.
  • Ministry of Health: led citizen research and co-design for Healthier SG, a national-level preventive health programme with over 1 million residents enrolled.
  • NUS: Valedictorian at the Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences (cohort of ~2,000 students), Psychology with a Sociology minor, Honours with Highest Distinction.

I occasionally write, share AI safety ideas in Chinese on Rednote, and code little web apps for fun.
