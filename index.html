About Outside work

Hi, I’m Yue.

I’m a policy researcher from Singapore, now based in Berkeley.

I care about keeping human agency as AI becomes more capable, and helping institutions recognise risks and act early.

Currently

  • Research fellow at SPAR and the Odyssean Institute.
  • Studying international AI coordination, expert judgments of AI risk, and citizen deliberation.
  • Exploring research on AI-driven gradual disempowerment.

Previously

  • Ministry of National Development: conducted research for national-level housing and retirement policies; piloted AI-assisted scenario planning research workflows.
  • Ministry of Health: led citizen research and co-design for Healthier SG, a national-level preventive health programme with over 1 million residents enrolled. Featured in DotGrid, Singapore Product Design’s zine (Pg 19).
  • NUS: Valedictorian at the Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences (cohort of ~2,000 students), Psychology with a Sociology minor, Honours with Highest Distinction.

I occasionally write and share AI safety ideas in Chinese on Rednote.
