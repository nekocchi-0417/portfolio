Reading list

People, policy, design, and a little fiction.

  • Doing Good Better

    William MacAskill

    Using evidence to turn good intentions into meaningful impact.

  • Good Services

    Lou Downe

    What makes a service functional, humane, and effective.

  • Hillbilly Elegy

    J. D. Vance

    A memoir about family, class, and the tension between personal agency and structural constraints.

  • Why Fish Don’t Exist

    Lulu Miller

    Natural history meets existential inquiry: how our need for order can reveal and distort truth.

  • How to Know a Person

    David Brooks

    On deep listening, curiosity, and helping people feel seen.

  • The Wedding People

    Alison Espach

    Love, friendship, and the performance of happiness, through the theatre of a wedding.

  • The House in the Cerulean Sea

    TJ Klune

    Chosen family, inclusion, and meeting bureaucracy with empathy.

  • Skin in the Game

    Nassim Nicholas Taleb

    On risk, responsibility, and who bears the consequences of decisions.

  • Abundance

    Ezra Klein & Derek Thompson

    On building more of what people need, from housing to clean energy.
